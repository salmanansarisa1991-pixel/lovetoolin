Invoice Kya Hota Hai?

Invoice ya Bill ek legal document hai jo seller buyer ko deta hai. Isme saman ka naam, quantity, rate, GST, total likha hota hai. India me 2 tarah ke bill hote hai: 1. Estimate / Proforma - quotation ke liye, 2. Tax Invoice - GST law ke hisab se final. Hamara tool Estimate ke liye 100% safe hai.

India Me Kitne GST Lagte Hai?

  • 0%: Doodh, sabji, kitaab, hospital, school
  • 0.25%: Rough diamonds
  • 3%: Gold, Silver, Jewellery
  • 5%: Kapde 1000 tak, transport
  • 12%: Mobile, processed food
  • 18%: Laptop, TV, most services
  • 28%: Luxury car, bike 350cc+, AC, cement
  • Cess: Luxury pe extra 1% to 22%

CGST/SGST/IGST Me Fark?

  • CGST+SGST: Same state (MP to MP) - 18% hai to 9% CGST + 9% SGST
  • UTGST: UT me SGST ki jagah
  • IGST: Other state (MP to Delhi) - pura 18% IGST
