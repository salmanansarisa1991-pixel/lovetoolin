1. Zameen Ki Units Kya Hai? Khet Me Kya Use Hota Hai?

India me zameen 2 tarah se naapi jati hai. City / Construction me - Sq Ft, Sq Meter, Gaj (Sq Yard), Marla, Kanal. Gaon / Khet me - Bigha, Acre, Hectare, Guntha, Cent. 1 Acre = 43560 Sq Ft = 4046.86 Sq Meter = 4840 Gaj. 1 Hectare = 2.471 Acre = 10000 Sq Meter.

2. Bigha Ka Chakkar - Sabse Important (State Wise)

  • MP / Chhattisgarh Bigha: 1 Bigha = 12000 Sq Ft = 1333.33 Gaj = 0.275 Acre. Jabalpur, Bhopal, Indore me yahi chalta hai.
  • UP / Delhi Pucca Bigha: 1 Bigha = 27225 Sq Ft = 3025 Gaj = 0.625 Acre = 2529 Sq Meter.
  • Rajasthan Bigha: 1 Bigha = 27489 Sq Ft = 3054 Gaj. Kuch jagah 17424 Sq Ft bhi mante hai.
  • 1 Guntha = 1089 Sq Ft = 121 Gaj = 1/40 Acre. Maharashtra, Karnataka me use.
  • 1 Cent = 435.6 Sq Ft = 48.4 Gaj = 1/100 Acre. South India me use.
  • 1 Marla = 272.25 Sq Ft = 30.25 Gaj. 1 Kanal = 20 Marla = 5445 Sq Ft. Punjab, Haryana me use.
