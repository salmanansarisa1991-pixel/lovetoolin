EMI Calculator - What is EMI? Full Guide in Hindi

EMI (Equated Monthly Installment) matlab har mahine bank ko fixed paisa dena. Isme 2 cheez hoti hai - Principal (asal loan) aur Interest (byaj). Shuru me Interest jyada hota hai, baad me Principal jyada kat-ta hai.

EMI Ka Formula Kya Hai?

EMI = [P x R x (1+R)^N] / [(1+R)^N - 1]
P = Loan Amount, R = Monthly Interest Rate, N = Total Months. Hamare calculator me N = (Years x 12) + Months. Isliye tum 20 Year 3 Month jaise bhi daal sakte ho.

Car Loan Bhi Isi Se Calculate Hoga Kya?

Haan, 100%. Home Loan, Car Loan, Bike Loan, Personal Loan sab ka formula same hai. Bas Interest Rate aur Tenure alag hota hai. Car Loan me aam taur par 11-13% interest aur 5-7 saal ka tenure hota hai. Home Loan me 9-10% aur 20-30 saal. Upar Loan Type select karoge to sab auto set ho jayega.

Year + Month Jodne Ka Fayda

Normal calculator me sirf Year ya Month me se ek dalta hai. Hamare me tum 1 Year 6 Month = 18 Month aise daal sakte ho. Saath me Start Date daaloge to End Date automatic niklega. Jaise Loan 10 Jan 2026 se shuru hai, 5 Year 3 Month ke liye hai to Khatam hoga 10 April 2031 ko.

EMI Kaise Kam Kare?

1. Har saal 1 EMI ka Prepayment karo - 20 saal ka loan 16 saal me khatam. 2. Downpayment jyada do. 3. Kam interest wala bank chuno. 4. Tenure kam rakho agar EMI afford kar sakte ho.
